Customer Support Triage Agent

ID: agent-cs-001 | Version 1 | 2026-08-18 | Advisory Authority LIMITED RISK

Purpose & Scope

The agent triages customer support tickets by reading ticket content, classifying urgency, retrieving customer profiles, and drafting advisory responses for human review. It operates with advisory decision authority, requiring human approval for all draft replies and enforcing escalation protocols for high-urgency or legal-related inquiries. The agent accesses confidential Zendesk data and PII from the Customer CRM to support these workflows.

Regulatory Basis

ISO 42001 Annex A.9.2 — information provided to users shall accurately describe system purpose

Decision Authority

Advisory

The agent provides recommendations but a human decision-maker must approve every action.

Regulatory Basis

EU AI Act Art. 14(1) — high-risk AI systems shall enable effective human oversight commensurate with their decision authority
EU AI Act Art. 14(4) — deployers shall be able to decide not to use or to override the AI system's output
NIST AI RMF GOVERN 4.1 — organizational policies shall define the level of human agency for each AI decision type

Human Oversight Mechanisms

  • A human support agent must review and approve every draft reply before it is sent to the customer.
    Trigger: Every ticket response
  • Escalations flagged as high-urgency are routed to a senior agent within 15 minutes.
    Trigger: Urgency score >= 8 or keyword 'legal'
